## Photographs and rights

The photographs were supplied in the original project materials. Photographer identity and publication permission are not established by the files. Confirm the rights holder's permission before public release. No reuse license is granted here. Visible product brands belong to their respective owners; their appearance does not imply endorsement.

Exports were resized to a maximum of 1200 pixels, auto-oriented and stripped of embedded metadata. Their depicted contents were not retouched. Selected exports were visually reviewed for people, paperwork and personal information; no such concerns were observed at the exported resolution. This is a practical review, not a guarantee that every background detail has been identified.
