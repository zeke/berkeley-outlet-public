# Keeping the guide useful

For a stock update, record the date, item, and how availability was confirmed. Mark sold items clearly. Keep historical photographs labeled with their observation date. Avoid presenting camera timestamps as independently verified dates.

Only add photographs you have permission to publish. Review the actual export for people, reflections, personal paperwork, addresses, account information and identifying labels, then strip embedded metadata. Keep private contact details, account workflows, copied reviews, speculative offers and internal planning materials out of this repository and its history.

Use the public store address for location context. Publish hours, prices, delivery terms or closing dates only after verification with the business, with the verification date. Correct or remove inaccurate material promptly.
